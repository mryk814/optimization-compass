## Contract

- Published concepts require a 35-character summary, 1,200-character body, four table-of-contents entries, and a valid internal link under `次に読む`.
- The concept floor is a hard publication gate.
- Sentence length, comma density, meta prose, and legacy headings are review warnings.
- Author work reports, over-precise pasted numbers (five or more decimals), and runs of 6+ unconnected one-sentence lines are review warnings.
- Existing warnings remain visible without failing unrelated changes.
- `ready content` rejects warnings on the article being prepared, so changed articles do not add new prose debt.
